Our position

On data

We do not sell behavioral data, viewing histories, or household patterns to anyone. We do not have a partnerships team whose job is to monetize your Tuesday night. What you watch stays where it belongs.

On pricing

There is one price: free, for family. We will not introduce an ad-supported tier to recover the margin we lost when we removed ads we never had. We will not send a letter explaining that the new pricing better reflects the value you receive.

On accounts

Your account does not require a credit card, a linked phone number, or consent to terms that quietly reserve the right to change the terms. You were given a username. It works. That is the entire relationship.

On features

We do not quietly remove features and re-introduce them at the Premium tier. We do not A/B test your interface without disclosure. The play button will remain in a location you can find without a tutorial.

Matneco Industries is a private technology company operating out of Central New York. Our products are developed for household use and are not publicly distributed. Any resemblance to a legitimate multinational conglomerate is entirely aspirational and we stand by it.
